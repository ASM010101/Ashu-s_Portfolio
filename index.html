About Ashish Maurya: Applied AI Engineer

My Engineering & Research Journey

My name is Ashish Maurya, and I am an Applied AI Engineer with deep expertise in LLMs, Speech AI, RAG, and Agentic Systems. I earned my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML) from SRM Institute of Science and Technology (2021–2025) with a CGPA of 9.04 / 10.00, and am pursuing my Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Patna (2026–2028).

At Samespace (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026), I served as an SDE-1 AI/ML Engineer, building production RAG pipelines with FAISS, fine-tuning foundation models for tool-calling, architecting multi-agent systems on Google ADK with custom MCP servers, and developing multilingual TTS with speech-to-speech accent translation.

Prior to Samespace, I was a Research Fellow under the prestigious IndiaAI Mission grant (Sep 2024 – Feb 2025) designing the ICIP framework for controllable Stable Diffusion generation, and a Research Fellow at HyperVerge Nexus (Feb 2024 – Jun 2024) focusing on synthetic ID generation and document forgery detection.
